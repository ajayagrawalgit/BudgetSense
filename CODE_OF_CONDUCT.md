# Code of Conduct

BudgetSense is a small, friendly project, and I want everyone who takes part to
feel welcome and respected.

## The short version

Be kind. Assume good faith. Disagree about code, not about people.

## What is expected

- Treat others with respect, whatever their background or level of experience.
- Give and accept feedback graciously. Critique the idea, not the person.
- Keep discussion focused and constructive.
- Respect that maintainers give their time voluntarily, and that "no" is a
  complete answer.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other channel connected to BudgetSense, and it applies when you are representing
the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these rules, please open a
confidential report by contacting the maintainer through the GitHub profile
linked in the README. Reports will be handled discreetly and in good faith.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this code, and
may warn or, if needed, block people who do not stop.
