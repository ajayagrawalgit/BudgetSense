## Why BudgetSense

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-cloud-off-outline:{ .lg .middle } __Offline first__

    ---

    Every number you enter stays on your own phone in a local database.
    Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly turn on backup.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md)

-   :material-shield-lock-outline:{ .lg .middle } __Private by design__

    ---

    No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Optional cloud backup is
    end-to-end encrypted with a passphrase only you know.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Security](SECURITY.md)

-   :material-notebook-outline:{ .lg .middle } __Calm, not clinical__

    ---

    No red numbers, no guilt trips, no upsells. A warm, paper-inspired design
    that treats you like a person, not a suspect.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Design system](DESIGN.md)

-   :material-backup-restore:{ .lg .middle } __Your data, recoverable__

    ---

    Full on-device backup and restore in JSON, CSV, and XML, plus optional
    encrypted Google Drive sync you control.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Data recovery](DATA_RECOVERY.md)

-   :material-cellphone-cog:{ .lg .middle } __Home screen widgets__

    ---

    Glance at your balance, log a quick expense, or check where the month is
    heading without ever opening the app.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Full spec](SPEC.md)

-   :material-open-source-initiative:{ .lg .middle } __Open source__

    ---

    The complete source code is public and licensed under the GNU GPL v3.0.
    Read it, build it, contribute to it.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Contributing](contributing.md)

</div>
