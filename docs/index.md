<section class="bs-scene bs-scene--tint" id="bs-journal" markdown>
<div class="bs-scene__inner" markdown>

<h2 class="bs-fade">It is really just a notebook</h2>

<p class="bs-oneline bs-fade">You open it, you jot down what came in and what went out, and you close it. Over a few weeks those little notes turn into the honest shape of your month.</p>

<div class="bs-cards" markdown>

<div class="bs-card bs-fade" markdown>
<span class="bs-card__icon">&#9998;</span>
### Jot it down in seconds
One sheet for money in, money out, investments, and transfers between your own accounts. Start typing "coffee" and it quietly finds the right icon for you.
</div>

<div class="bs-card bs-fade" markdown>
<span class="bs-card__icon">&#8635;</span>
### The repeating stuff repeats itself
Rent, subscriptions, SIPs, EMIs, the yearly insurance you always forget. Tell BudgetSense once. Mark it paid and the entry writes itself, then the date moves along on its own.
</div>

<div class="bs-card bs-fade" markdown>
<span class="bs-card__icon">&#128176;</span>
### Loans and investments, in one place
Loans quietly count down what you still owe. Your SIPs and investments sit alongside your spending instead of in a separate app you forget to open.
</div>

<div class="bs-card bs-fade" markdown>
<span class="bs-card__icon">&#128065;</span>
### Yours alone, at a glance
Tap the eye and every number blurs. Unlock with your own fingerprint, face, or PIN. Screenshots are blocked by default.
</div>

</div>
</div>
</section>

<div class="bs-cards" markdown>

<div class="bs-card bs-fade" markdown>

Every number lives on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you decide it should be.

<div class="bs-card bs-fade" markdown>

### Private by design
No account. No ads. No analytics. Nothing to collect, because I collect nothing.

<div class="bs-card bs-fade" markdown>
<span class="bs-card__icon">&#128215;</span>

No red alarms, no shame, no upsells. A warm, paper-inspired design that feels like a good notebook.
</div>

<div class="bs-card bs-fade" markdown>

Full on-device backup and restore. Optional cloud backup is encrypted with a passphrase only you know.
