## 1. What BudgetSense is

BudgetSense is an offline-first personal finance journal for Android and iOS. It
helps you record and reflect on your own income, spending, investments, recurring
bills, and loans. All data is stored on your device. There is no BudgetSense
account and no BudgetSense server. An optional Google Drive backup can be turned
on by you, in which case your data is encrypted on your device before upload.
